    TRANSPARENCY & ASSURANCE

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about our custom architectural building process and guarantees.

    How does your fixed-price contract protect against cost blowouts?

    Every detail—from subterranean engineering and concrete structural quantities to specific European tiles and Sub-Zero appliances—is fully documented and priced upfront into a Master Builders Australia (MBA) fixed lump-sum contract. We do not use vague provisional sums, ensuring total budget certainty.

    Can we customize an existing floorplan or design completely from scratch?

    Yes, absolutely. You can either select from our curated portfolio of custom designs and adapt the layout to your lifestyle, or collaborate directly with our in-house architectural team to design a one-of-a-kind home tailored to your block’s contours, solar orientation, and council zoning.

    What is the typical construction timeframe for a custom luxury home?

    Depending on the home’s square meter footprint and topography (such as basement excavations or cliffside foundations), our luxury builds typically take between 12 to 18 months. We station a full-time Senior Construction Director on-site to ensure strict milestone adherence.

    Do you manage municipal Council DA and CDC approvals?

    Yes. Our in-house town planners and town planning silks handle all council submissions, heritage assessments, Bushfire Attack Level (BAL) zoning, and private certifier liaison from start to finish.

    What statutory warranties and structural guarantees are provided?

    All residences are backed by our 10-Year Statutory Structural Warranty, exceeding standard National Construction Code regulations. We also offer our 2-Year White-Glove Concierge Maintenance service for continued peace of mind.
