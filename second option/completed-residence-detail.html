The Balmoral Travertine Pavilion

Perched on a 38-degree natural sandstone shelf overlooking Balmoral Beach, this three-level residence incorporates monolithic Roman travertine porticos, post-tensioned concrete cantilevers, and an 18-meter mineral infinity pool.

The Commission Brief

Harmonizing Heavy Masonry With Unobstructed Ocean Vistas

The client acquired a heritage-adjacent sloping site with world-class views across Middle Harbour and Sydney Heads. The mandate was clear: construct a private sanctuary capable of hosting 100 guests that felt intimate, quiet, and thermally inert year-round.

To counteract extreme coastal salt spray, JS Construction engineered all structural steel with 316-grade marine passivation, cast post-tensioned concrete with crystalline waterproofing additives, and detailed double-rebated European thermal breaks.

Engineering Benchmarks Achieved

  • ✓ 28 Bored Rock Piers: Anchored 9 meters deep into solid Hawkesbury sandstone.
  • ✓ Zero Variations: Contract signed at $3,450,000; delivered at exact contracted figure.
  • ✓ Passive Thermal Envelope: 7.8-star NatHERS energy rating via cross-flow breezeways.
