Optimized for Steep Incline Coastal Lots

The Bronte Horizon 58 solves one of Sydney’s most notorious challenges: building on a narrow, sloping block without losing privacy, natural light, or garden space. The stepped floorplate cascades down the site naturally.

A vertical central light void pierces all three levels, flooding the subterranean media lounge and wine gallery with midday sun while acting as a natural thermal stack for cross-ventilation.
