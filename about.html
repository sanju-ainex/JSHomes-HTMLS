SYDNEY MASTER BUILDERS • LUXURY RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION

Turning Your Vision Into Bespoke Architectural Reality

Headquartered at Berry Street in North Sydney, JS Construction Sydney Pty Ltd brings together visionary architectural design, premium residential building, and direct personal care. Specializing in bespoke custom homes, knockdown rebuilds, and smart dual occupancy developments across Greater Sydney, we deliver every home with fixed-price certainty, open communication, and master craftsmanship.

OUR BUILDING PHILOSOPHY

Where Smart Design Meets Daily Family Comfort

We believe a luxury home should be easy to understand and effortless to live in. It is not just about square metres; it is about high ceilings that let in natural morning light, quiet bedrooms where you can rest, and spacious living kitchens where your family gathers naturally.

Whether we are constructing a knockdown rebuild in Panania or Revesby, a custom coastal home in Cronulla or Vaucluse, or a modern family estate in Epping, our team manages every detail. You speak directly with your builder, receive clear milestone updates, and enjoy complete peace of mind with guaranteed fixed-price contracts.

OUR CORE SPECIALIZATIONS

Built On Four Proven Services

From single custom residences to multi-dwelling developments, we guide you from concept drawings to your front door key.

01 / CUSTOM HOMES

Custom Home Building

Bespoke architectural residences designed around your lifestyle, family routine, and block orientation. We do not use rigid cookie-cutter plans; every detail is personalized for you.

02 / REBUILDS

Knockdown Rebuilds

Love your Sydney suburb, schools, and neighbours, but outgrown your aging house? We safely demolish your existing home and build a brand-new luxury home on your existing block.

03 / DUAL LIVING

Dual Occupancy & Duplexes

Unlock the hidden financial value of your block. We design and build side-by-side or multi-generational duplex residences that create high rental yield and massive capital equity.

04 / DEVELOPMENTS

Townhouses (Class 1 & 2)

Expert project delivery for boutique townhouse developments. We streamline council development applications (DA) and complying development certificates (CDC) to keep builds on track.
